I remember that some day I tought about the tv show: "the rookie", in one of the epesodes there was a device hidden in a puplic trash can, it was a simulatad bomb that insted of exploding on you it shoots pouwder on you. S I thought to myself: "hmmmm why won't I make one my self" and then my brain started braining and thinking of ideas for how can I do it? Eventully I thought to make it as an April fools parnk, that shoots glitter on you. but how would it know if you're even there and it actually shoots the glitter on some one? So the sollution for it was adding a dictance sensor. when the someone gets close to the device it opens a door, poppes a bloon and on the bloon there is glitter so it makes it pop at you.🎉
#

**How it workes?**
When the Time Of Flight sensor (the dictance sensor) detects something, it openes a door controled by a servo connected with a pushrod. After the door is opend there is a rack and pinion mechanism that controls a pin, when the pin is all the way out it pops the ballon and shoots the glitter.
